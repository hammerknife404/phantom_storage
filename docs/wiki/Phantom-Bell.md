# Phantom Bell

Every pet at once. Unlocked by *Hot Tourist Destinations*.

| Charm use (bell mode) | Result |
|---|---|
| Use | Summon every unlocked pet that isn't out |
| Sneak + use | Dismiss every following pet and close your [gateway](Phantom-Gateway) |

Anchored pets (an anchored [anchor](Phantom-Anchor) or [collector](Phantom-Collector)) stay where they are either way, since dismissing one forgets its spot; dismiss those in their own mode. The bell rings either way.
